Zikap is operated by Highlink LLC.

A proposed refund approach and the current payment workflow, awaiting owner decisions.

Service termsPrivacy noticeCancellation & refunds

1. Proposed approach for owner approval

Proposed, not adopted:

Before agreed work starts, cancel the paid project and refund the full project payment. After work starts, review any refund against the agreed scope, completed work and the reason for the request; explain any proposed partial amount in the project conversation before processing it.

The owner has not yet approved this approach. No fixed refund window, guaranteed percentage after work begins or no-refund rule is being introduced by this draft.

2. How a customer would request review

  1. Open the corresponding project in your customer account or use the original receipt-based tracking route.
  2. For account-linked projects, use the project conversation to state the order reference and explain the cancellation or service issue. Do not send card numbers, passwords or receipt keys in the message.
  3. Zikap reviews the accepted scope, payment record and current work stage, then records the outcome and any agreed next step.

A public support contact is still needed for guest customers or anyone unable to sign in. It must be completed before this policy is adopted.

3. Unpaid requests and incomplete checkout

Submitting a project request does not charge you. If no payment was completed, there is no completed project charge to refund. Closing a checkout page is not a refund action; the saved project and Stripe record are used to determine payment status.

A pending checkout can be checked or expired through the existing payment workflow. Do not make repeated payments merely because a browser page is slow or a return screen is unclear.

4. Accepted refund decisions

Financial refunds are initiated by the merchant in Stripe. A project note, delivered status, cancelled request or action-plan update does not itself move money. A refund cannot exceed the payment amount that was collected.

After a refund is processed and its event is received, the Zikap project can show the recorded refund status. Use the payment provider’s financial record if the project status is delayed or needs investigation. Partial refunds and complete refunds are distinct.

5. Where the money goes and timing

An approved refund is processed against the original payment through the payment provider. Bank/payment-method processing and failed or pending refund outcomes can affect when it appears. No exact arrival date is promised by this draft.

The merchant may still incur provider costs even when it refunds a customer. This draft does not authorize deducting fees from a customer’s refund.

6. Delivery questions, duplicate payments and test mode

If a delivered report needs clarification, the included scoped revision route may resolve the issue. Asking for clarification does not itself waive any mandatory right. A duplicate or unrecognized payment should be identified through the corresponding project and Stripe record.

Payments explicitly labelled Stripe test mode are simulated. They do not charge real funds, and a test-mode refund does not return real money.

7. Decisions still required

  • Approve or change the proposed full refund before work starts and case-by-case review after work starts.
  • Complete the registered business details for Highlink LLC and a public contact route for refund requests.
  • Confirm how work commencement is documented with the customer and any applicable mandatory consumer requirements.

Nothing in a final policy should exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. This draft does not select a jurisdiction or claim to determine every customer’s statutory rights.
