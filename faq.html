BEFORE YOU START

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do you guarantee a #1 Google ranking?

No. Rankings depend on competition, your website, and search engine changes. The focus is on measurable improvements and transparent reporting.

What does the free tool check?

It checks ten basic signals in the HTML you provide. The score reflects passed checks in this tool only. It is not an official Google metric or a complete SEO audit.

How do I get a page’s HTML?

In a desktop browser, open the page and choose View page source, or use Ctrl+U. Copy the code into the checker. You can also upload a saved HTML file.

How much does a project cost?

Starter packages are $29 for a 5-page technical review, $19 for 25 keyword ideas, and $39 for a 3-page on-page edit brief. The combined starter bundle is $79. Prices are in USD, one-time. Broader work and delivery timing are agreed separately.

Where is my information stored?

The instant HTML checker runs in your browser. Content plans are saved in this browser only when you choose Save on this device; you can also download and reopen a backup file. Project details are stored for review and delivery only when the request service is available and you submit the form. New projects are linked to your signed-in customer account. Older projects still use their original private receipt until you link them to an account. Account sessions use protected cookies. Save your private account recovery code; email verification and recovery emails are not available yet.

Is Zikap part of Moz, Ahrefs, or Google?

No. Zikap is independent. Links to educational sources are for further reading and do not imply partnership, certification, or endorsement.

Can I order and pay online?

Create a customer account to submit and manage project requests. Your dashboard shows progress, reports and the included clarification/revision option. Earlier orders can be linked using their original private receipts. Acceptance, scope and timing still need agreement. After acceptance, Stripe checkout is available when enabled. Card details are entered on Stripe.

Will a high checker score guarantee a good ranking?

No. The score covers ten basic HTML checks only. It does not account for competition, content usefulness, links, live website behavior, or search engine decisions.

What information should I avoid in a brief?

Do not include passwords, payment details, API keys, or private account credentials. Describe the website and its needs using information appropriate for a project summary.
