BEFORE YOU START

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do you guarantee a #1 Google ranking?

No. Rankings depend on competition, your website, and search engine changes. The focus is on measurable improvements and transparent reporting.

What does the free tool check?

It checks ten basic signals in the HTML you provide. The score reflects passed checks in this tool only. It is not an official Google metric or a complete SEO audit.

How do I get a page’s HTML?

In a desktop browser, open the page and choose View page source, or use Ctrl+U. Copy the code into the checker. You can also upload a saved HTML file.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing and timing depend on page count, existing issues, and the agreed scope. Start by preparing a project brief to outline your needs.

Where is my information stored?

This version has no accounts, tracking, or server-side data storage. HTML analysis and brief creation happen in your browser. Downloaded files remain on your device.

Is Zikap part of Moz, Ahrefs, or Google?

No. Zikap is independent. Links to educational sources are for further reading and do not imply partnership, certification, or endorsement.

Can I order and pay online?

Online ordering and payment are not enabled in this version. The project brief downloads to your own device and is not sent to Zikap.

Will a high checker score guarantee a good ranking?

No. The score covers ten basic HTML checks only. It does not account for competition, content usefulness, links, live website behavior, or search engine decisions.

What information should I avoid in a brief?

Do not include passwords, payment details, API keys, or private account credentials. Describe the website and its needs using information appropriate for a project summary.

Give your next SEO project a clear starting point.

Outline your website, priorities, and preferred service. Download a brief to keep your project requirements in one place.
