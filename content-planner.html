PUT PURPOSE BEFORE VOLUME

A useful topic
needs a clear job.

  1. Start with real questions.

    Use customer conversations, your own search data, and the pages your website needs.

  2. Match the page to the intent.

    A practical guide, comparison, and service page answer different needs.

  3. Collect evidence before drafting.

    Add experience, reliable sources, and relevant examples to each brief.

  4. Review before publishing.

    Check facts, links, metadata, and the next step for the reader.
