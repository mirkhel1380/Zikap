Where do I find my receipt?

After submitting a request, stay on that page and choose Download private receipt. The text file contains your Order ID and Private receipt key. Check your browser downloads for a file beginning with zikap-order-.

If your submission page is still open, choose View my receipt above. A receipt created in another tab stays in that tab. Opening this page again does not restore it.

Your owner access key (ADMIN_TOKEN) does not work here. If the original receipt page was closed or refreshed and you did not download the file, its private key cannot be recovered in this version.
