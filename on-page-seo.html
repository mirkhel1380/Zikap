Make every page worth finding.

On-page SEO brings together the page’s purpose, the information it provides, and the way that information is organized. It helps readers recognize that they have arrived in the right place and makes the subject easier to understand.

A Zikap on-page project begins with a selected set of pages and their intended audiences. The aim is to make each page accurate, useful, and easy to navigate while giving it a clear relationship to the rest of your website.

What gets reviewed

Purpose & contentCheck whether the page answers its intended question, contains useful detail, and supports a relevant next step.
Titles & descriptionsDraft page-specific titles and summaries that describe the content clearly without repetitive keyword use.
Headings & readabilityOrganize sections so readers can scan, understand, and move through the page.
Internal linksIdentify natural connections to useful related pages, using descriptive link text.
Images & contextReview whether images support the content and whether their alternative text serves an appropriate purpose.

Title and description lengths are editorial guidelines rather than fixed ranking rules. Search engines may choose different titles or snippets. A tidy checklist score does not measure the full value of a page.

An example: a service page with too little context

A hypothetical page titled “Our Services” might list a service name but leave visitors unsure what is included. A more useful page could explain the customer problem, the scope of work, what the customer receives, and what information is needed to begin.

The improvements could include a more specific title, a clearer opening section, descriptive headings, useful related links, and answers to practical questions. This is an editorial example, not a claim about a customer result.

Edits with a reason behind them

  • Page-by-page recommendations tied to audience needs and the agreed target topics.
  • Suggested title tags and meta descriptions for the selected pages.
  • A proposed section outline and notes on gaps or unclear information.
  • Internal link recommendations and a review checklist for implementation.

Full copywriting, design changes, development, and publishing are included only when agreed in the scope. Recommendations can be handed to your existing team or used to define a separate implementation phase.

What to bring to the review

Identify the pages that matter most, what visitors should learn or do, and the facts that must stay accurate. Supply brand guidance, approved product or service details, and any supporting materials. Content should reflect what your business can actually deliver.

Is there a keyword density target?

No fixed percentage is used here. The priority is clear, natural language that answers the page’s intended need.

Should every page have more words?

No. Add detail when it helps the reader make progress. Length alone does not make a page more useful or guarantee better rankings.
