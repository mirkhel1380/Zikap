## Project and refund handling

1. Before accepting work, confirm the package, exact public pages, deliverables, necessary inputs and timing in the customer-visible project update. Use the stored project amount rather than a later catalog price.
2. Before starting paid work, record a dated scope/start update and the In progress status. Payment or a free check alone does not establish work commencement.
3. For cancellation/refund review, identify the customer/order through the existing authenticated conversation or another verified contact. Never ask for card numbers, passwords, recovery codes or private receipt keys in messages.
4. Apply the approved principles: full project-payment refund before work starts; documented unperformed portion after work starts; correction at no extra charge for a scope deficiency, then partial/full review if unresolved. Record completed work and explain the calculation. Do not deduct processing costs under this policy.
5. Initiate any approved financial refund through the merchant's Stripe workflow after verifying the actual charge and previous refunds. A support status or project update does not refund money. Check pending/failed outcomes separately, avoid duplicate reimbursement and record the financial result before saying it completed. Provider/bank timing is not guaranteed.

## Privacy requests and retention

1. Record receipt, scope, verification method, applicable deadline and any permitted extension notice in the existing private support case. Collect only the information needed to verify and fulfill the request.
2. Assess account/project/support/check information and the separate payment/accounting/dispute purposes. The account-download convenience feature does not delimit a statutory access request.
3. Determine which records can be corrected/deleted and which genuinely need continued retention. Explain the outcome and reason for any necessary retention. Application support status changes do not perform deletion. Do not mark deletion complete until the authorized underlying changes are verified.
4. Preserve the person's right to request reconsideration. Record the review and applicable appeal/complaint route. Apply mandatory deadlines where the relevant law covers the business, person and processing; registration in Texas alone does not resolve applicability.
5. Review retained records when their stated purpose ends. The application does not run a general timed account/project purge. Seven-day saved-check access expiry and operational row cleanup remain as described in the notice.
