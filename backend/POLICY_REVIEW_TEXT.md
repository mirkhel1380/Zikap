# Service terms

Operator: Highlink LLC, Texas, United States.



## 1. What Zikap provides

Zikap offers focused SEO reviews and recommendations, including technical SEO, keyword research and on-page edit briefs. The selected package, submitted page URLs, business topic, language, market and accepted project brief define the work. Check the current package page and the accepted project update before paying.

The existing starter packages are one-time projects in USD. They are not monthly subscriptions. A separately offered future service needs its own clear scope and price.

Current starter deliverables

 | Service | Included scope | Delivery

 | Technical review | One website, up to 5 public URLs | Prioritized findings and an action checklist

 | Keyword research | 25 keyword ideas for one topic, language and market; a map for up to 5 pages | A curated keyword plan, without live volume or difficulty figures

 | On-page briefs | Up to 3 existing pages on one website | Proposed metadata, content and internal-link improvements

 | Combined starter | The three scopes above for the same website | A combined specialist report

The current package comparison shows the saved catalog names and one-time prices. The confirmed project scope and stored project amount govern an accepted request; a later catalog edit does not rewrite that stored amount.

## 2. From request to accepted project

Submitting a request stores your brief for review and does not charge you. Acceptance is a separate step. Zikap confirms scope, necessary inputs and timing in the project update before work proceeds. Before paid work begins, Highlink LLC records a dated, customer-visible scope/start update and marks the project In progress. Payment alone does not start the paid work. A request may be declined or need a different scope.

Do not rely on an estimated delivery date until it is agreed for your project. Changes to URLs, topics or requested work may need a new agreement.

## 3. Your information and website access

Supply accurate contact details and only request checks of websites you are permitted to have reviewed. Share public URLs or appropriately limited, agreed information. Never put passwords, API keys, recovery codes, card details or other secrets in a brief or project message.

Public website checks sample source HTML. They respect access limitations and robots rules and may be unable to examine some websites. They do not execute page JavaScript or establish Google indexing, rankings, search traffic, backlink coverage or keyword volumes.

## 4. Payment and project status

When an accepted project has checkout available, review the agreed scope and amount before continuing to Stripe-hosted checkout. Card details are entered on Stripe. Zikap records payment references and status; returning from checkout alone is not proof that payment succeeded.

Payment and fulfillment are separate. Paying does not automatically create an SEO report, implement changes or publish content to your website. The merchant for Zikap is Highlink LLC; Stripe may show the merchant’s business name. Stripe test-mode records are clearly labelled and do not represent real charges.

## 5. Reports and clarification

Specialist reports are delivered through the customer workspace or the original private-receipt tracking route. Keep your login and receipt private. You can read or download a saved report and communicate about that project through the available project features.

The current starter scope includes one clarification or revision request within the original agreed work. An unsent clarification draft stays in the current tab across project refresh and reopening; it is not submitted automatically. Reloading the page or ending the account session clears it. Additional pages, new research topics, implementation or ongoing support require separate agreement. Customer-managed action-plan tasks record your own progress; marking one complete is not independent verification of a fix.

## 6. AI-assisted suggestions

Where available, AI explanations are a separate optional step after a website check and require consent to share the limited structured findings with the AI service. The workspace shows availability and usage limits. AI can produce inaccurate or incomplete suggestions, so review the original evidence before making a change.

AI explanations do not publish website changes, replace the accepted specialist scope or guarantee search results. A limited check with no findings does not certify a website as issue-free.

## 7. SEO outcomes and permitted use

Search visibility depends on many factors outside a single review. Zikap does not guarantee a particular ranking, traffic increase, indexing decision, AI-search recommendation or sales result. Recommendations should be assessed against the actual website and its business context.

Do not use the service to probe private networks, bypass website restrictions, access another customer’s projects, submit unlawful content or abuse the available quotas.

## 8. Cancellations and questions

Use the project conversation or Help & support to raise a service issue or request cancellation. In Payments, Refund review opens the support form with the corresponding topic; sending it starts a review. A message or status change does not itself process a financial refund. The cancellation and refund policy records the owner-approved approach: a full refund before work starts, refund of the unperformed portion after work starts, and correction followed by partial/full refund review for unresolved delivery issues. Read the complete cancellation and refund policy before paying.

The operator is Highlink LLC. Support and refunds: support@zikap.site. These terms do not waive mandatory customer rights or replace separately agreed project terms.

## 9. Your customer account

My account contains your saved name and sign-in email, security controls and private data download. You can update your display name; this does not change your email, credentials or older project records. Email ownership and recovery by email are not currently verified or provided by the application.

Keep your latest private recovery code or recovery file safe. Opening that file locally fills the recovery form; you still choose a new password and submit the form yourself. Successful account recovery replaces the recovery code and signs out existing sessions.

Security shows session creation/expiry dates and the current session. Closing other sessions requires your current password and affects their next authenticated requests. The list does not identify devices or provide a complete activity audit.

## 10. Materials and delivered recommendations

You retain your rights in materials you supply and authorize Zikap to use those materials to review, support and deliver your requested work. Supply material you are permitted to share. Delivered reports and recommendations may be used for your own website and business; any third-party materials remain subject to their applicable rights and permissions.

## 11. Questions and later changes

Keep project questions and agreed changes in the customer workspace so the scope, timing and response remain connected to the request. General account questions can be raised in Help & support. Support contact: support@zikap.site.

If adopted, this version would apply to new service arrangements from its confirmed effective date. It does not silently replace an already accepted project scope or price. Material project changes require separate confirmation, and applicable mandatory customer rights remain unaffected.


# Privacy notice

Operator: Highlink LLC, Texas, United States.



## 1. Who this notice concerns

This notice describes data handling in the current Zikap website, customer workspace and separate owner portal. Zikap is operated by Highlink LLC. Privacy contact: privacy@zikap.site.

## 2. Information the service processes
- Accounts: name, email address, salted password hash, hashed recovery information, session records and account timestamps. Account email ownership is not currently verified.
- Projects: website/page addresses, package and stored price, topic, language, market, goals, status, reports, project messages, read acknowledgements, revision requests and customer-managed action plans, support request subjects/topics/statuses and their conversations, plus owner-only internal review checklists and notes.
- Payments: Stripe checkout/payment references, amount, currency, mode, recorded payment/refund status and processing timestamps. Card details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout rather than in Zikap’s project forms.
- Website checks: requested public URLs, sampled HTML observations and evidence. Account-saved checks can also include optional AI suggestions and usage reservations.
- Request metadata: network metadata processed by the hosting service and application rate-limit counters using hashed address-derived keys. These support operation and abuse prevention.

## 3. Why this information is used

Information is used to manage accounts and access, review project requests, confirm agreed scope, deliver work, support project conversations, record payment events, operate website checks and apply safety and usage limits. The current service does not provide a public directory of customers or reports. The current Zikap application has no personal-data sale feature, advertising-network integration or automated eligibility decision feature.

Specialist project information is visible to the authorized owner through the separate administration workspace. A private receipt can also authorize access to its corresponding project, so it must be kept secret.

## 4. Hosting, payments and optional providers
- GitHub Pages serves the public website and its assets. Its infrastructure processes visitor requests under GitHub’s own terms and privacy practices.
- Cloudflare hosts the account/API and independent owner applications and the D1 database. It processes requests and stored service records.
- Stripe processes checkout/payment information. Its privacy practices also apply to Stripe-hosted checkout and its payment records.
- Optional Cloudflare Workers AI: the separate AI-consent step shares only finding IDs, fixed check codes, severity/category and human-review flags. The current prompt construction excludes raw page text, website addresses, names, account details, reports, messages and receipt keys. Suggestions and usage reservations are saved in the customer’s private review record.
- Resend, when transactional email is activated: sending uses a recipient address, configured sender/reply address and a short notification containing an order reference, package title or payment amount. Reports, messages, names, page URLs, passwords and receipt keys are excluded from the templates. Sending is a separate configuration step.

Service operation and authorized owner access, as well as provider processing, can involve handling information outside your country. This notice does not promise storage in a single country. Questions about a provider, data location or transfer arrangement can be raised through the privacy contact for review in relation to the relevant service.

## 5. Cookies and browser-local tools

The customer workspace uses an essential secure session cookie with a seven-day lifetime. The owner portal uses a separate session cookie. Signing out revokes the current session; password replacement or account recovery revokes existing sessions. My account also lets you inspect session creation/expiry dates and, after password confirmation, close other sessions. This is not device identification or a full sign-in audit.

Browser-local HTML checking and local CSV analysis do not send the pasted file contents through those local tools. A URL-based website check is different: the server requests the public pages. The content planner stores a plan on your device only when you choose its device-save option. Device copies and downloaded files are under your control and are not removed by signing out.

Opening a Zikap account recovery file reads it locally and fills the email and masked recovery-code fields. The file itself is not uploaded. Recovery details are sent to the account service only when you submit the recovery form.

Unsubmitted project and clarification drafts are held in the current tab’s memory. Workspace navigation can preserve them; page reload or account cleanup clears them. They are sent to the account service when you submit the corresponding form.

The current public site does not include Zikap advertising pixels or a Zikap marketing analytics integration. Hosting and checkout providers can process their own operational information.

## 6. Storage and retention

Retention principles: Highlink LLC keeps account and project information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain account access, deliver agreed support, and address relevant payment or service issues. Keeping an account active is not a blanket reason to retain every record indefinitely. Records are reviewed when those purposes end or a deletion request is received.
- Private AI-review checks expire from customer access after seven days. Expired rows are purged when another account-saved check is created; this is not a promise of physical deletion at an exact minute.
- Account sessions expire after their stated lifetime. Expired sessions and rate-limit records are cleaned through service operations.
- Order history retains up to twenty previous saved states per order. That is a rolling recovery feature, not a complete backup.
- Necessary payment, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute records are assessed separately. Where retention is required for an applicable obligation or unresolved issue, Highlink LLC retains only the information needed for that purpose and reviews whether continued retention remains necessary. No single accounting retention period is asserted for every record.
- Started notification attempts can store the rendered recipient/sender payload and provider acceptance reference. Provider acceptance does not establish inbox delivery.

Current operation: the application has no general automatic account/project deletion schedule. Retention and deletion reviews require manual handling. Some payment-linked records are protected from ordinary order deletion. Submitting or resolving a support request does not automatically erase an account, a project or a payment record.

Downloaded files and browser-local copies remain under the customer's control; closing a server account or signing out does not remove those device copies. Provider-held records are also subject to the relevant provider's own retention obligations and practices.

## 7. Questions and data requests

Use My account / Privacy requests to open Help & support with the Privacy or account data topic selected. You can request access, correction or deletion without a paid SEO order. You may also contact privacy@zikap.site without creating a customer account. Include enough information to identify your request; avoid unnecessary personal details.

Highlink LLC reviews the request's scope, verifies that it concerns the requesting person, and considers any necessary payment, legal or dispute-related retention before deciding what can be removed or corrected. A signed-in support request helps identify the account. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, receipt keys, payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents in a message.

The outcome and any reason for retaining relevant records are explained through the verified contact channel. Request handling is manual; a support status is not proof of completed deletion. The existing account download feature described below does not erase data. There is no self-service account-deletion control or automatic export covering every possible record.

Depending on the law that applies to you and the processing involved, rights may include confirmation/access, correction, deletion, a portable copy and objections or restrictions on certain uses. We review requests and any necessary verification under the applicable rules and response deadlines, including any permitted extension notices.

If you disagree with a response, ask for reconsideration in the same verified support conversation or contact privacy@zikap.site, identifying the original request and what you want reviewed. Where an appeal or a further complaint route is required by applicable law, we explain that route in the response. For Texas rights that apply, the appeal response includes how to raise an unresolved matter with the Texas Attorney General. The rights and deadlines that apply depend on your location and the relevant processing.

## 8. Account details and private downloads

In My account / Personal details, you can update your saved display name and view the account creation date. Other corrections can be requested through the Privacy topic; a name update does not rewrite older project records.

My account / Your data lets a signed-in customer re-enter the current password and prepare a private JSON file. It includes the profile, currently linked project briefs and reports, project conversations, customer action plans, recorded payment summaries, unexpired saved website checks and account support requests with their conversations. Private owner work, security credentials, receipt keys, processor secrets and browser-only tool data are excluded.

The authorization lasts up to ten minutes and is tied to the same login session. Only its hash is stored. The file is assembled in the browser. Its prepared download link has a separate ten-minute lifetime after the file is ready. Clear prepared file revokes that browser link; page/account cleanup or preparing another download also clears it. In-page workspace navigation alone does not unload the page. Files already saved on your device remain under your control and are not deleted by clearing the link. Zikap does not create a stored server archive. It is a collection across successive reads, so concurrent record changes can affect the result. A download does not erase an account or change a payment. The current file limit is 50 MiB and 1,000 records; an incomplete preparation does not offer a completed file. The convenience download and its exclusions do not limit the scope of a separate privacy-rights request, which is reviewed manually.

## 9. Information about children

Zikap is designed for people arranging SEO work for websites and businesses. Do not include children’s personal information in website briefs, reports or support messages. If you believe such information has been provided, contact privacy@zikap.site so the issue can be reviewed.

## 10. Changes and contact

This notice is a review draft and has no effective date. Updated versions identify their effective date. Material changes to features, providers or the processing described here should be reflected in an updated notice. Privacy contact: privacy@zikap.site.


# Cancellation and refunds

Operator: Highlink LLC, Texas, United States.



## 1. Refund approachProposed approach for future service arrangements, subject to adoption:
- Before agreed work starts: cancellation of a paid project receives a full refund of the project payment.
- After work starts: the portion of the payment attributable to work not performed is refundable. Highlink LLC explains the completed work, the agreed scope and the calculation of any partial refund in the customer-visible project conversation before processing it. No arbitrary fixed percentage is used.
- After delivery: if the delivered work does not match the agreed scope, Highlink LLC first offers to correct that issue within the agreed scope at no additional charge. If the issue remains unresolved, a partial or full refund is reviewed according to the undelivered or deficient work and the seriousness of the issue.

This approach does not limit any mandatory customer rights. Refund review is based on the agreed work and its actual stage; bank arrival times are outside Zikap’s direct control.

### When paid work begins

Before beginning paid work, Highlink LLC records a dated, customer-visible project update confirming the scope and start of work and marks the project In progress. The customer can read that update in the dashboard. Payment, acceptance of a request or running a free website check alone does not establish that the paid project work has started.

The existing dashboard status and project update fields support this process. They do not automatically start work, calculate a refund or transfer money.

## 2. How to request cancellation or refund review
- Open the corresponding project in your customer account or use the original receipt-based tracking route. In Payments, choose Refund review to open the support form with that topic selected.
- Use the project conversation or the support form to state the order reference, what you want cancelled or reviewed, and the reason for your request. A support request can be opened without an existing linked project. Do not send card numbers, passwords or receipt keys in the message.
- Zikap reviews the accepted scope, payment record and current work stage, then records the outcome and any agreed next step.

Support and refund contact: support@zikap.site.

## 3. Unpaid requests and incomplete checkout

Submitting a project request does not charge you. If no payment was completed, there is no completed project charge to refund. Closing a checkout page is not a refund action; the saved project and Stripe record are used to determine payment status.

The project payment-status control can check a pending checkout. An expired checkout is not proof of a collected payment. Do not make repeated payments merely because a browser page is slow or a return screen is unclear.

## 4. Accepted refund decisions

Financial refunds are initiated by the merchant in Stripe. A project note, delivered status, cancelled request or action-plan update does not itself move money. A refund cannot exceed the payment amount that was collected.

After a refund is processed and its event is received, the Zikap project can show the recorded refund status. Use the payment provider’s financial record if the project status is delayed or needs investigation. Partial refunds and complete refunds are distinct.

## 5. Where the money goes and timing

An approved Stripe refund is sent back to the original payment method used for the charge. Bank/payment-method processing and failed or pending refund outcomes can affect when it appears. The time taken depends on the payment method and processing by the bank or provider.

If a refund is recorded as pending or failed, ask for a review through the corresponding project or support conversation. Do not treat a pending record as proof that the money has reached your account.

The merchant may still incur provider costs even when it refunds a customer. Provider processing costs are not deducted from the customer refund described in this policy.

## 6. Delivery questions, duplicate payments and test mode

If a delivered report needs clarification, the included scoped revision route may resolve the issue. Asking for clarification does not itself waive any mandatory right. Report a suspected duplicate or unrecognized payment through the corresponding project or support request so Highlink LLC can review it against the Stripe record. Do not submit another payment to resolve a suspected duplicate.

Payments explicitly labelled Stripe test mode are simulated. They do not charge real funds, and a test-mode refund does not return real money.

## 7. Questions, changes and mandatory rights
- If adopted, this version would apply to new service arrangements from its confirmed effective date. Existing agreed project arrangements and mandatory rights are preserved.
- Future versions will identify their effective date. A later revision does not silently alter the agreed scope or stored price of an existing project.
- Use the dated project-update process above to document work commencement and account for any applicable mandatory consumer requirements.

Nothing in this policy excludes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Mandatory rights may differ according to the customer’s location and the service arrangement.
