## Primary-source research

Read on November 8, 2026:

- FTC privacy/security guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security — supports matching privacy representations to actual practices.
- FTC data-minimization/retention guide: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business — retain information needed for legitimate purposes; not a blanket fixed retention period.
- Texas Attorney General TDPSA overview: https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-privacy-rights/texas-data-privacy-and-security-act — notice/request/appeal considerations, exemptions and online email routes. Registration in Texas alone does not establish applicability; SBA small-business status and relevant markets/processing were not independently determined. No universal 45-day or global-law promise was inserted.
- Stripe refund documentation: https://docs.stripe.com/refunds — original-payment-method destination, partial/full and pending/failed distinctions, and total refund limits.
- Stripe refund fees guidance: https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-fees-for-refunded-payments — merchant processing costs do not justify silently promising fee deductions from customer refunds.

These sources informed drafting. The text is not a provider compliance certification, legal opinion or proof of statutory coverage.
