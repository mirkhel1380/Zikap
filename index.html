BEFORE YOU START

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do you guarantee a #1 Google ranking?

No. Rankings depend on competition, your website, and search engine changes. The focus is on measurable improvements and transparent reporting.

What does the free tool check?

It checks ten basic signals in the HTML you provide. The score reflects passed checks in this tool only. It is not an official Google metric or a complete SEO audit.

How do I get a page’s HTML?

In a desktop browser, open the page and choose View page source, or use Ctrl+U. Copy the code into the checker. You can also upload a saved HTML file.

How much does a project cost?

Starter packages are $29 for a 5-page technical review, $19 for 25 keyword ideas, and $39 for a 3-page on-page edit brief. The combined starter bundle is $79. Prices are in USD, one-time. Broader work and delivery timing are agreed separately.

Where is my information stored?

The instant HTML checker runs in your browser. Content plans are saved in this browser only when you choose Save on this device; you can also download and reopen a backup file. If the project request service is connected, submitted project details are stored for review and delivery. A private receipt key protects access to updates and reports.
