Where should you start?

Pages are hard to findStart with technical SEO when access, indexing signals, navigation, or URL behavior needs investigation.
Unsure what to publishStart with keyword research when your audience, topics, and page plan need definition.
Pages need clearer contentStart with on-page SEO when important pages exist but need better structure, context, and internal links.

How the services work together

Technical review addresses access and delivery. Research clarifies the audience’s needs. On-page work turns that understanding into useful pages. The sequence depends on the site: an urgent access issue may come first, while a new content project may begin with research.

A combined project should still have explicit deliverables, responsibilities, and review points. Website size, access, and the type of changes determine the scope; pricing and delivery dates are agreed before work begins.
